\begin{abstract}
Prompt-level interventions can substantially reduce security vulnerabilities in
LLM-generated code, yet turning prompt edits into reliable engineering
guidance faces two coupled bottlenecks in multi-operation programs:
(i)~\emph{confounded observational proxies}---surface cues such as audit
docstrings or file headers correlate with secure outputs through latent or
environment confounders while delivering zero causal gain under explicit
intervention---and (ii)~\emph{multi-operation scope bleed}---applying even a
verified security rule as a flat module-wide instruction corrupts sibling
operations that share data objects or standard-library primitives.

We introduce \model, a two-stage framework coupling Task-Grounded Semantic
Graphs (\prompttsg) with Joint Causal Inference (JCI) mechanism discovery and
within-task paired counterfactual evaluation. In Stage~I, \prompttsg builds a
tolerant span-grounded representation over task operations, nested
\texttt{part\_of} object ownership chains, logical conditions, and normative
propositions while degrading unanchorable facts into explicit
\texttt{unresolved} boundaries; extracts an 8-predicate orthogonal structural
mechanism vector; and renders operation-scoped interventions decoupled from
AST code splicing and paired with target-operation-scoped AST security
auditing. In Stage~II, under strict split isolation
($D_{\mathrm{disc}} \cap D_{\mathrm{eval}} = \emptyset$), \model discovers a
JCI-12 Partial Ancestral Graph over exogenous environment/intervention contexts
$C=(C_{\mathrm{env}}, I)$ and post-generation temporal outcome sinks
$(Y_{\mathrm{sec}}, Y_{\mathrm{func}})$ to separate true causal security drivers
from confounded proxies and unscoped scope-bleed hazards, evaluating policies on
held-out tasks via within-task paired counterfactual intention-to-treat (ITT)
contrasts.

Across 48 independently verified multi-regime validation cells (4,538
conditional independence tests), \model achieves 100\% abstention (0/12 false
positives versus 12/12 for correlation selection) on spurious-only negative
controls, improves joint secure-and-functional yield by +0.127 to +0.147 over
correlation policies and +0.095 to +0.283 over unscoped flat rules, and cuts
unpaired estimator variance by 56.5\%--82.9\%. In an independently verified
formative evaluation over 24 multi-operation Python legacy-maintenance tasks (96
live generations across six CWE families on \texttt{Qwen2.5-Coder-32B-Instruct}),
\model attains 91.7\% (22/24) joint success (100\% security, 91.7\%
functionality), outperforming unguided baseline generation (29.2\%,
$\hat{\Delta}=+0.625$, $p=6.1\times 10^{-5}$), correlation-guided scoped prompts
(0.0\%, $\hat{\Delta}=+0.917$, $p=4.8\times 10^{-7}$), and unscoped flat
causal rules (12.5\%, $\hat{\Delta}=+0.792$, $p=3.8\times 10^{-6}$) with zero
per-task regressions.
\end{abstract}
