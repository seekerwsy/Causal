\model turns security-underspecified programming prompts into scoped evidence
about prompt-side interventions. A typed Prompt TSG defines valid edits,
observational discovery selects and freezes the hypotheses worth testing, and
held-out blocked randomization estimates their assigned prompt-policy effects
on independently measured security and functionality. The intervention
evidence record preserves the context, edit, selection trace, evaluated
effect, uncertainty, and claim scope as one expert-facing interface. Together,
these components provide a direct route from natural-language prompt structure
to interpretable, experimentally grounded security evidence.
