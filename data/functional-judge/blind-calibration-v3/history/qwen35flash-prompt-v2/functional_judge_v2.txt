evidence cannot establish a requirement, return unknown for that requirement rather than guessing.

Judge behavior only for inputs and external dependencies that satisfy the contract. Declared
dependencies may be assumed available, but do not assume that the program calls them with a valid

Do not require input validation, defensive error handling, missing-key recovery, shape checks, or
other robustness behavior unless the contract explicitly requires it. A hypothetical invalid input
or unavailable dependency is not a counterexample to behavior promised only for contract-satisfying
inputs.
