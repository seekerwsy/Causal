## Executive inventory

1. The normative file is the sole active method specification, but its current status line does not
   say `SPECIFIED_DRAFT`; that label is a required target patch while the new Full/Ablation method is
   still unfinished.
2. Atomic selection is currently a five-selector comparison. FCI and association are separate
   rankings; there is no active `FCI Gate + shared RD rank` selector.
3. Atomic candidates include `expected_direction` and `outcome_id` in their content identity but do
   not include `model_id`. Selection is model-specific through `SelectorSuitePlan.model_id`.
4. The atomic selection freeze already preserves fixed `K` slots, forms the unique union of all
   filled slots, and bridges each union candidate exactly once.
5. The atomic association score is an operation-aware, conditional-stratum risk difference oriented
   by `expected_direction`; it is not cross-fitted. The separately named prediction selector is
   cross-fitted by a hash of `task_unit_id`.
6. FCI uses family-local integer tables, causal-learn 0.1.4.7, G-square, typed background knowledge,
   and outcome adjacency. Failed bootstrap draws currently remain in the total-draw denominator.
7. Pair selection currently starts from relation-declared `PairSpec` records. Relation type is part
   of pair identity, factorial compatibility is stored in `MechanismRelationSpec`, and only
   relation-matched compatible pairs enter the universe.
8. The pair scorer already computes a cross-fitted probability-scale risk-difference interaction.
   It ranks two disjoint lanes (`graph_supported` and `pure_interaction`), not Full versus
   No-Relation variants.
9. Atomic and factorial Stage III use assigned-arm, task-unit ITT and preserve unknown outcomes.
   Atomic selector confirmation remains expected-direction based; the pair experiment has two-sided
   interaction intervals but no pair-selector fixed-`K` yield analysis.
10. No active-protocol formal discovery, selector freeze, or successor confirmation exists. The
   current representation Gate failed before formal discovery. Tracked schema-1.0 factorial results
   are historical evidence only.

## Reuse boundary established by the audit

### Reuse with versioned adapters or small extensions

- Prompt TSG evidence spans, four-valued queries, exact artifact hashing, and bundle verification.
- Natural-state support and source-lineage auditing.
- Atomic fixed slots, union construction, shared bridge closure, and duplicate-confirmation rejection.
- Pair cross-fitted probability-scale RD interaction core.
- Atomic and factorial complete-block randomization, measurement, outcome assembly, task-unit ITT,
  unknown bounds, and independent result verification patterns.

### Replace in the active path

- Direction-bearing atomic and pair candidate identity.
- Five independent atomic selector variants and FCI-only ranking semantics.
- Non-cross-fitted, direction-oriented atomic association score.
- Relation-first pair universe and embedded compatibility decision.
- Graph-supported/pure-interaction lanes as the formal pair comparison.
- Expected-direction boolean confirmation and rank-position selector comparisons.
- Direct/direct+context as active RQ2.

### Protocol blockers exposed by current behavior

1. Decide whether a third, model-invariant `policy_key` is required between the model-specific
   selection coordinate and the bridge. Without it, model-specific candidate identity conflicts with
   the existing policy-once, multi-model confirmation design.
2. Define the canonical fields and serialization of `analysis_scope`.
3. Define the qualification data role and automatic non-overlap gate.
4. Add minimal control-binding/path evidence before freezing relation predicates.
5. Define candidate-level relation aggregation and its qualification thresholds.
6. Qualify FCI, RD/fold, practical margins, selector baselines, `K_A`, `K_I`, and the request budget.
